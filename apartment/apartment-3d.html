    Shortcuts and tips

    Drag the background to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Double-click a room to fly to it.
    Click a piece to select it, then drag it; it snaps to walls (hold Alt for free 1 cm moves). Click a floor to change its finish, a window to add curtains or blinds.
    R rotate 90° · Shift+R back · arrows nudge 1 cm (Shift 10 cm) · ⌘/Ctrl+D duplicate · Del remove · Esc deselect · ⌘/Ctrl+Z undo.
    Changes save automatically in this browser. Use Export to keep a copy.
